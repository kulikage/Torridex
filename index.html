The Technology

TorrideX bypasses the thermodynamic limits of traditional RTGs by harvesting energy across three distinct domains, preventing ionization energy from degrading into waste heat.

Isotope Source ⟶ Plasma Environment ⟶ Triple Cascade Extraction ⟶ High-Efficiency Power

1. The Chamber

Radioisotope-induced non-equilibrium plasma within a modular sealed chamber (cylindrical, toroidal, or annular). No combustion, no complex moving parts.

2. E×B Transport

Magnetic fields perpendicular to electric fields accelerate electrons to 400 m/s, suppressing recombination and generating a powerful Hall current.

3. Triple Cascade

Energy is completely harvested via direct electrostatics (DC), inductive AC coupling, and advanced thermoelectrics for unprecedented system efficiency.
